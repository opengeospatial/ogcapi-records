[[per_stac-extensions_assets]]

[permission]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /per/stac-extensions/assets
part:: A record MAY include an `assets` member at the top level of the record object. If present, its value SHALL be an object that maps unique keys to https://github.com/radiantearth/stac-spec/blob/v1.1.0/commons/assets.md[STAC Asset Objects].
part:: A catalog MAY include an `assets` member and an `item_assets` member as defined for https://github.com/radiantearth/stac-spec/blob/v1.1.0/collection-spec/collection-spec.md[STAC Collections].
part:: Members that a STAC extension defines for assets MAY be placed on the Asset Objects of a record or catalog.
====
