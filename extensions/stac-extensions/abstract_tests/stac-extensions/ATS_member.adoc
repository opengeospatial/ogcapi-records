[[ats_stac-extensions_member]]

[abstract_test]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /conf/stac-extensions/member
target:: /req/stac-extensions/member
test-purpose:: Validate that records and catalogs that use STAC extensions list them in the `stac_extensions` member.
test-method::
+
--
. Retrieve each record and each catalog.
. Identify the members that are defined by STAC extensions. Members of STAC extensions usually have a name with a prefix followed by a colon, for example `proj:code`.
. If such members exist, verify that the record or catalog includes a `stac_extensions` member at the top level.
. Verify that the value of the `stac_extensions` member is an array of strings without duplicates.
. For members of the STAC extensions registered at https://stac-extensions.github.io/, verify that the array contains the schema URI of the corresponding extension. Members of other extensions cannot be attributed to an extension reliably, because their prefixes are not registered.
--
====
